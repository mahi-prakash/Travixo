## 🐞 [BUG-001] Planner Page: AI-Suggested Itinerary Activity Timings are Uneditable

- **Status:** Open
- **Priority:** Medium
- **Component:** Frontend (`Planner` page / "Your Plan" section)
- **Reported Date:** 2026-08-02

### Description
In the Planner page under the **Your Plan** itinerary section, the timings for activities that are suggested by the AI are currently locked / uneditable. 

### Expected Behavior
Users should be able to click into and edit the timing schedules of any itinerary activity suggested by the AI to customize their trip plans.

### Current Behavior
The activity timings generated by the AI plan are currently read-only and cannot be modified by the user.
